What is SD IMOP?

  1. SD IMOP: San Diego Infrastructure Management Optimization Platform.
  2. SD IMOP is a data-driven platform designed to optimize infrastructure maintenance across San Diego.
  3. By harnessing historical repair records, citizen-reported issues, and facility condition assessments, SD IMOP leverages machine learning to predict when and where maintenance is needed.
  4. The goal is to enable proactive repair scheduling, reduce overall costs, and enhance public safety.
